About Me

I am an interior designer with a special interest in residential, retail, hospitality, and nautical projects. Based in Milan, I graduated from the Politecnico di Milano with a Master's degree in Interior and Spatial Design.

Throughout my studies, I developed a deep passion for creating spaces that blend functionality with aesthetic beauty. My work focuses on transforming environments into thoughtful, liveable places that reflect the personality and needs of their inhabitants.

I believe that good design should be both beautiful and practical — every detail matters, from the choice of materials to the play of natural light.
