Eterna

Project in collaboration with SanLorenzo

Lower Deck — Retreat

The lower deck has been designed to offer a true retreat from the outside world, focusing on privacy, tranquillity, and personal wellbeing. It features a master suite with panoramic sea views, a spacious bathroom, a VIP cabin, and two twin berths. From here, a secluded space dedicated to reflection, quiet mornings, and personal luxurious pursuits.

Main Deck — Passion

The fly deck embodies the spirit of freedom, a place where open skies and panoramic views set the tone for total relaxation. This level offers a private lounge area for sunbathing and an outdoor dining space, ideal for breezy lunches or intimate dinners at sunset.

Upper Deck — Togetherness

The upper deck is dedicated to togetherness, blending practicality with comfort to create a seamless space for connection, from quiet mornings to elegant evenings.
