Hidden in the Unhidden

University project

The design centres on creating peaceful, restorative spaces that offer refuge from the demands of daily life. Quiet corners, natural materials, and soft lighting work together to foster calm and contemplation throughout the interior.

In contrast, areas dedicated to social vibrancy encourage interaction and community. Open layouts, communal zones, and dynamic design elements bring people together, creating a lively atmosphere that celebrates connection and shared experiences.
