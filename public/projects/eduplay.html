EduPlay

University project

The Exterior

Surrounded by lush greenery, the building has been designed with simplicity in mind, drawing inspiration from its predecessor to ensure continuity in architectural style.

The cream-coloured structure is complemented by light wooden fences at both the ground level and along the upper floors, creating a sense of lightness and fluidity that softens the solid block design. At the beginning of the pathways, colourful arches invite visitors, guiding them toward the entrance and creating a welcoming and playful atmosphere.
